Delete Account

Users of ST ENGINE can request permanent deletion of their account at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open ST ENGINE.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

If you cannot access your account, you may request account deletion by contacting:

Email: support@stcbroker.id

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account will be permanently deleted.
  • Your personal information will be deleted from our systems, subject to any information we are legally required to retain.
  • This action cannot be undone.
